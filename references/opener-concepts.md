# Opener Concepts — Structural Blueprints (KinetRig Engine)

Read before drafting openers, product promos, feature teasers, or commercial intros BEFORE generating code.

Without deliberate conceptual framing, openers for different products inevitably converge on the identical cookie-cutter structure: zoomed text exiting left, three feature icon tiles, a typed search bar, and a generic hook → feature → feature → logo sequence.
This catalog guarantees structural novelty across every deliverable.

---

## Structural Governance Laws

1. **Three Candidates, One Pick:** Propose three distinct conceptual blueprints from the catalog below with single-sentence premises and palettes before writing code.
2. **Structural Fingerprint:** Document: `concept · scene count · hero subject per scene · opening shot · climax`.
3. **Canonical Component Cap:** Standard tropes are permitted only if demanded by the narrative, capped at a maximum of TWO per opener:
   - Zoomed copy flying offscreen left;
   - Three identical feature tiles in a horizontal row;
   - Typed input search bar;
   - Push-through camera transition;
   - Specular light leaks at cut points;
   - Centered logo + CTA lockup as final frame.
