# Storyboard Sequences (`references/sequences.md`)

Pre-engineered narrative blueprints and timing structures for KinetRig. Eliminates guesswork for AI coding agents when structuring 15s to 60s video narratives.

---

## 1. Blueprint: SaaS Product Launch (30 Seconds)

- **Style Recommendation:** Cinematic Dark or Editorial Light
- **Pacing:** High initial hook → steady feature walkthrough → climactic CTA

```markdown
[0.0s - 3.5s] ACT I: The Bottleneck (Problem)
- Camera: Low-angle Dutch tilt (rz: -6 deg), slow forward crawl.
- Visual: Monolithic headline "MANUAL WORKFLOWS ARE BREAKING", red alert strobe.
- Audio: Low sub-bass rumble, vinyl needle scratch cut.

[3.5s - 12.0s] ACT II: The Solution Architecture (Hero Product)
- Camera: Whip pan 90 deg right, leveling to eye-line (rz: 0 deg).
- Visual: Persistent 3D laptop mockup tumbling into frame (see references/mockups.md).
- Animation: Live dashboard data table renders; counter rolls from 0 to 14,800 ops/sec.
- Audio: Crisp mechanical keyboard clicks, rising synthetic chord.

[12.0s - 22.0s] ACT III: Three Pillar Innovations (Feature Cascade)
- Camera: 2.5D Orbit sweeping right (ry: 15 deg) through depth layers.
- Visual: Bento box grid pops sequentially (Recipe 2.3).
- Feature 1: "Instant Zero-Config Setup" (SVG terminal icon).
- Feature 2: "Sub-Millisecond Edge Sync" (Glowing ray beam).
- Feature 3: "Encrypted By Default" (Lock shield unmasking).
- Audio: Snappy percussive beat drops on each card arrival.

[22.0s - 27.0s] ACT IV: Quantitative Proof (Metric Milestone)
- Camera: Wide pedestal crane upward (y: -300).
- Visual: Massive typography "99.99% FASTER", shimmering neon glow.
- Audio: Cinematic brass impact with sub-bass drop.

[27.0s - 30.0s] ACT V: Brand Landing & Call to Action
- Camera: Clean push-through to center logo lockup.
- Visual: Company logo vector draw, domain URL, "Get Started Free" button.
- Audio: Sustained synth tail fading to black.
```
